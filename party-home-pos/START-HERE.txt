PARTY & HOME POS

OPEN YOUR WEBSITE
Double-click Start POS.cmd. It starts the app in the background and opens
http://localhost:3000 in your browser. Node.js 22.13 or newer is required;
the Node.js version installed on this computer is supported.
No package downloads are needed.

FIRST USE
1. Create your owner account with a username and a password. There are no default passwords.
2. Enter your shop name, contact details and address.
3. Add products in Products & stock: picture, unique SKU, department, selling
   unit, opening stock, low-stock threshold and P1 through P5 selling prices.
4. The owner and supervisors can enter and view cost prices. Sales staff
   cannot retrieve costs from the server, including through the API.
5. To add staff, open Settings & access. Enter their username, role and password.
   Existing staff roles/passwords can be changed using the pencil button.

SELLING
Select a price level and click product pictures to add items to the order.
Quantities and unit prices can be edited manually. Changing the price level
applies that level's price to every inventory item already in the order.
Use Add a manual item for services or other untracked descriptions.

A quotation is a sales order and does not reserve or deduct inventory.
An invoice deducts quantities immediately when saved. For example, stock
of 10 packs becomes 7 after invoicing 3 packs. Stock cannot go negative.
Manual items do not affect product stock.

Saved quotations can be converted into an invoice once, using the quoted
prices. Repeated saves cannot deduct the same invoice's stock twice.
Invoices are final records and cannot be edited/deleted. This version has
no refunds, credit notes or invoice cancellation workflow.

View and print any document from Sales documents. Your browser's print
dialog can save a PDF. Cost prices never appear on customer documents.
The payment method/terms field records your choice; the app does not
process a payment or track payment balances. All prices are in ZAR.
VAT is off by default. In Settings, enter your own VAT registration number
and the VAT included in your selling prices if applicable. Entered prices
remain the final totals; included VAT is shown as a breakdown on documents.

STOCK AND REPORTS
Use the boxes button in Products & stock to add stock or record a signed
adjustment with a reason. Existing stock is not overwritten when editing
product details. Supervisors can archive/restore products without deleting
their history. Stock activity shows the most recent 200 movements.

Sales reports show invoice revenue, invoice counts, units sold per day,
sales trends and bestsellers over 7, 30 or 90 days. Export creates a CSV.
Quotations are excluded from sales figures. Dates use South African time.

DATA AND ACCESS
Records and account password hashes persist in data/pos.sqlite. Uploaded
pictures live in data/images. Keep a backup of the entire data folder.
Stop the app before copying a backup, or include the SQLite -wal/-shm files.
Signing out expires your session. Sessions also expire after eight hours.
Passwords are salted and hashed. Do not delete the data folder to restart.

PHONE AND COMPUTER
The app listens on your local network so phones and computers on the same
shop Wi-Fi can use the same stock and sales database. Keep this computer
on with the app running. On the phone, open http://192.168.8.43:3000 and
sign in with a team account. This address can change if the router assigns
a different IP; run ipconfig to find the computer's current IPv4 address.
Windows Firewall may need to allow Node.js on your private network.
The computer browser can continue using http://localhost:3000.
Set POS_HOST=127.0.0.1 to restrict the app to this computer again.
Internet hosting has not been completed. Local network access does not
make the app available over mobile data or outside the shop network.

DEVELOPMENT
node server/local.mjs       Start in a terminal (Ctrl+C stops it)
node --test tests/pos.test.mjs   Run inventory and permission tests
node tests/http.test.mjs        Run real HTTP/authentication tests
node scripts/build-portable.mjs Build a Cloudflare Worker under dist/
node tests/browser.mjs          Chrome browser checks where supported

Core files: web/app.js, web/style.css, server/api.js, server/database.js,
server/local.mjs, server/schema.sql. The hosted Worker uses Sites-managed
ChatGPT authentication and the DB/BUCKET bindings declared in
.openai/hosting.json. The local app uses its own local accounts.

Usernames and passwords have no character rules or minimum length.
Both fields must be filled in.
Usernames are not case-sensitive. Existing email-based accounts can still
sign in using their previous email as the username and their existing password.

DEPARTMENTS
Open Departments in the sidebar. Enter all department names, one per line,
and click Save departments. Existing product categories are automatically
listed as departments. Choose a department from the dropdown when creating
or editing a product. You can also add departments from that product form.
Use the Department filter in Point of sale and Products & stock to show
only items from one department. Select All departments to show everything.
Unused departments can be removed from the Departments page.

INVOICING AND SALES ORDERS
Use Invoicing or Sales orders in the sidebar for the transaction layout.
Enter customer details at the top, use Add stock (F4) to find items, then
edit quantities and prices in the line table. Comments and totals are below.
On phones, line items become cards with touch-friendly quantity controls.
Process invoice or Save sales order opens a review before saving.
Ctrl+Enter also opens the review. Sales orders are stored as quotations
and can be converted to invoices from Sales documents.

CUSTOMERS AND FASTER ENTRY
Use Customers to add or edit account codes, names, phone and email,
billing and delivery addresses, VAT numbers, payment terms and price levels.
Choose customer in an invoice or order to fill these details. Selecting a
customer applies their default price level to existing inventory lines.
Customer History shows documents linked to that saved customer.
Owners and supervisors can archive customers; historical records remain.

Type or scan a product SKU into Stock code / barcode, enter a quantity,
and press Enter. Exact codes add immediately; other searches open the
product picker. F4 opens the picker, and Enter selects its first result.
Enter from a line quantity moves to price; Enter from price moves to SKU.
Totals update while valid quantities and prices are typed.
Stock is checked before reviewing invoices and again when saved.

Drafts are saved in this browser for the signed-in user and restored after
refresh. Drafts do not sync to other browsers or reserve inventory.
Use Copy to new on a saved document to start a repeat sales order with the
same items and prices. Save or clear your current draft first.

PRINTED INVOICES AND SALES ORDER NOTES
Use Save & print in the review dialog to save, then select your printer in
the browser print dialog. Save invoice / Save sales order opens a preview
without automatically printing. The Print / Save PDF button can reprint any
saved document. Select A4 and disable browser headers/footers if enabled.
Long documents have numbered continuation pages and final-page totals.
Sales order notes are marked NOT FOR DESPATCHING because they do not
reserve or deduct stock. Invoice conversion preserves the quoted details.

Enter your shop's address/contact details and Print settings before use.
Print settings support your own banking details, VAT number, optional
included VAT rate, and payment instructions. No supplier details or QR
codes from the reference photographs have been copied into your documents.
New documents capture customer, shop and print details as saved snapshots.
Changing those details later does not change a previously saved document.
